# Third-party material

The ARM7TDMI interpreter comes from
[mGBA](https://github.com/mgba-emu/mgba), via
[nokia-dct3-emulator](https://github.com/djr-747/nokia-dct3-emulator).
Its MPL-2.0 license, copyright headers, revision records, and integration notes
are retained in [third_party/mgba-arm](third_party/mgba-arm/README.md).
The combined emulator includes original GPL-3.0-only code. Under
[MPL 2.0 section 3.3](https://www.mozilla.org/en-US/MPL/2.0/#distribution-of-a-larger-work),
the vendored C sources and headers are additionally distributed under
GPL-3.0-only as part of that combined work. Their SPDX identifiers are
`MPL-2.0 OR GPL-3.0-only`; their existing MPL and copyright notices remain intact.

The mathematical cipher parameters and Phoenix container format used by
`nokia1208.py` come from [g3gg0/DCT4Crypt](https://github.com/g3gg0/DCT4Crypt).
Inferred ROM services and reset-state information draw on
[g3gg0/TriX](https://github.com/g3gg0/TriX).

The Nokia firmware and TDS6 binary in `firmware/` are copied unchanged from the
local project inputs. They retain their original ownership. Neither the project's
GPL grant nor the CPU core's MPL license grants rights to these binaries.
No separate license grant for the firmware was present in the supplied files.

The handset image `assets/nokia1208_chassis.png` is the existing project's crop
of a Pixabay photograph. The supplied project did not record the photograph's
author or original page URL. `assets/rh105_factory_nvram.bin` is the project's
factory-reset seed, generated by running the firmware, rather than saved user
phone storage. Neither asset is included in the project's GPL grant: no
relicensing permission for the photograph or firmware-derived seed is established
by the supplied inputs.

Each firmware binary and binary asset has an adjacent `.license`
sidecar with `SPDX-License-Identifier: LicenseRef-No-License-Provided`.
[The corresponding record](LICENSES/LicenseRef-No-License-Provided.txt) explains
that this means missing licensing information, rather than a license grant.
The binary files themselves are unchanged.
